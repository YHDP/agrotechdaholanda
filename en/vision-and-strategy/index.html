Vision and strategy

The farmer at the centre of water, energy and food.

Agriculture accounts for about 70% of the fresh water drawn worldwide, and it puts food on everyone's table. We help you use every drop as sparingly as a Dutch farmer would, and harvest more. We think it through with you and test it in the field.

Source: FAO AQUASTAT. Worldwide, 69% of freshwater withdrawals go to agriculture.

It all starts with the grower.

You decide when to irrigate, when to harvest and when to sell. We bring the tools: a pump that runs on the sun, irrigation guided by the soil, a cold room to keep the harvest.

  1. Solar arrayNo fuel no waiting for the grid.

Water touches other things that matter: energy and food. At the centre of it all is you, the grower.

Water, food and energy are one problem on the farm

Pumping water costs energy. Irrigation secures the harvest. Storing the harvest costs energy again. We work where the three overlap.

Whoever looks after the water looks after the harvests to come.

The river that runs through your land has passed other farms first, and runs on to others after. In a drought, everyone feels it. Measuring the water, irrigating only what the plant needs and never clearing forest to do it: that is how the land regenerates and the farm keeps producing.
