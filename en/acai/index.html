Why water

On upland ground, drought costs the harvest

Where the climate has 3 to 5 dry months, Embrapa recommends supplementary irrigation for upland açaí. The harvest runs from July to December and the off-season from January to June, varying with the location (Embrapa, 2014).

  • 3 to 5 dry monthsThat is when Embrapa recommends irrigating upland açaí. In north-east Pará, the dry months run from August to November.
  • Up to 120 litres per clumpA day, in the dry season, in a mature orchard with 400 clumps per hectare (Embrapa, 2019).
  • Irrigate before it runs outMany growers only start once water is already short. Embrapa names this as one of the main reasons for low yields in Pará's orchards.
  • 12.9 t/hais what BRS Pai d'Égua reaches with irrigation and fertiliser, in its eighth year (Embrapa, 2019).

Smart solar irrigation

Micro-sprinklers powered by the sun

Micro-sprinklers are the most used method on upland açaí. The solar system pumps from a well, a river or a reservoir and starts when the soil needs it.

  • Where the grid does not reachIn 2017, a third of farms in north-east Pará had no electricity (IBGE). The solar pump does not depend on the grid.
  • Led by the soil, not the calendarIn the complete systems, sensors measure moisture, and irrigation starts before water runs short.
  • A harvest in the off-seasonWith irrigation, 44 to 46% of BRS Pai d'Égua fruit comes in the off-season, January to June (Embrapa, 2019).
  • From 1 to 10 hain the reference systems; larger areas case by case.

Solar cold room

A few more hours before pulping

Açaí fruit must be pulped within 24 hours. At around 10 °C, that window stretches (Embrapa). The solar cold room holds the picked fruit until pulping or transport.

  • Around 10 °CWithin the cold room's range, which runs from −5 °C to +18 °C.
  • On the farmA 6 or 12 m (20 or 40 ft) container, with the sun first.

Açaí on land already open, not in forest

Embrapa recommends planting upland açaí on land already in use and avoiding untouched areas. On the floodplain, intensive açaí management reduces tree diversity (Freitas and others, 2021, with the Museu Goeldi). That is why our focus is upland ground.

Frequently asked questions

Does upland açaí need irrigation?

Where there are 3 to 5 dry months, yes: Embrapa recommends supplementary irrigation for upland açaí. In north-east Pará the dry months run from August to November, and a bearing palm cannot go more than two weeks without water: it drops its fruit. With irrigation, BRS Pai d'Égua gives 44 to 46% of its fruit in the off-season. On the floodplain, the river waters the palms.

How much water does an upland açaí orchard need?

Embrapa gives, per clump per day in the dry season, 40 litres in the first year, 60 litres in the second and third, and 120 litres from the third year on, at 400 clumps per hectare. Micro-sprinklers are the most used method. The right system for your area comes in the proposal, sized to the water source and the terrain.
