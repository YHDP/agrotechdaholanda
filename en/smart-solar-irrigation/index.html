How it works

Irrigates when the soil asks for it

Panels, pump and controls work as one system. In the complete systems, sensors measure the soil and the weather, and the control box starts the pump at the right moment.

  • Solar panelsGenerate the pump's energy, with no diesel and no grid.
  • Solar pumpSubmersible in the borehole, or a surface pump in the river or pond.
  • Solar pump inverterDrives the pump directly with the panels' energy.
  • SensorsMeasure the soil, the water and the weather, in the complete systems.
  • Control boxStarts the pump when the soil asks for it.
  • AppYou follow along and operate it from your phone.

Power ratings, flow rates and the options (valves, hydrocyclone, fertigation, battery): in the tech sheet.

Frequently asked questions

How does the solar pump for irrigation work?

The solar panels feed a solar pump inverter, which drives the pump directly, with no diesel. The pump can be submersible, in a borehole, or a surface pump, in a river or pond. In the complete systems, the control box starts the pump when soil moisture asks for it.

Does it irrigate by itself?

In the complete systems, yes. The control box reads the sensors and starts the pump when the soil asks for it, instead of following a fixed calendar. You follow along and can operate it from your phone.

What does the system measure?

In the complete systems, sensors measure the soil, the water and the weather. What each sensor measures is in the tech sheet.

What size of area is it for?

The reference systems go from 1 to 10 ha. Larger areas, up to 30 ha per pump, are sized case by case.

Do I need a permit to take the water?

Yes. Taking water in Brazil requires a water-use permit (outorga) from the state or federal authority. The permit is the farmer's responsibility, and we help with the process from the first conversation.

How much does it cost?

There is no price on the site. The price varies with the area, the water source, the total head, the crop and the options, and comes in a detailed proposal, with no obligation.

What does diesel cost me today?

It depends on your area, your crop and the price you pay. The calculator (in Portuguese) does that sum with your own numbers.

Is a system already installed in Brazil?

Nothing is installed in Brazil yet. Dutch Flow Technology runs a system in San José, in the Philippines. We are looking for the first farms for a pilot; see how it works.
