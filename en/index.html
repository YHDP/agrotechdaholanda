The nexus

Water, food and energy are one problem on the farm

Pumping water costs energy. Irrigation secures the harvest. Storing the harvest costs energy again. We work where the three overlap.

  • water and energyWe design the water system, not just sell the pump.From rainwater catchment to replacing the diesel pump, with the site chosen using a water-risk map of all 5,570 municipalities (in Portuguese).
  • water and foodData that helps finance it.The sensors send their readings as they happen; the data is yours, and a bank can read the same numbers. With banks, we look for new ways to finance small farmers and cooperatives.
  • food and energyWe learn together, in the field.The technology is still developing. Every part of the system can be reconfigured to answer a research question, and it is with researchers and the first pilots that we learn how it behaves in Brazil.

Solar cold room

Harvest, chill on the farm, and sell when the price is right.

A container that arrives ready by truck, with a cold room and a technical bay. The cold comes from the sun first; at night, the battery holds some hours, and the grid or the generator covers the rest.

  • From +10 °C to −5 °C
  • Arrives by truck and can be moved
  • Production in 6 to 8 weeks
  1. Sun: by day, the panels cool the room and charge the battery.
  2. Battery: at night, it holds the cold for some hours.
  3. Grid or generator: takes over by itself when the battery reaches its minimum.

Each product has its own temperature and humidity setting. The full table is in the tech sheet.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a grid connection?

For irrigation, no: the solar pump works by day, on the sun, and needs no grid connection. The solar cold room also uses the sun first, but its installation includes a grid or generator connection as backup for the night. How that works hour by hour: the cold room at night. For irrigation, a grid-tie inverter is an option for when there is not enough sun (tech sheet).

How much does solar irrigation or the solar cold room cost?

There is no price on the site: the price of solar irrigation and of the solar cold room comes in a detailed proposal, with no obligation. For irrigation it moves with area, water source, total head, crop and options; borehole, installation, freight and import are quoted separately, and payment is 50% at order and 50% before shipment. For the cold room it moves with size, options, freight, import and installation. The PDF tech sheet comes by e-mail, with no obligation. Ask for a proposal or see what you save on diesel (calculator, in Portuguese).

If it breaks, who repairs it? Are spare parts available in Brazil?

Today there is no service network of its own in Brazil: nothing is installed here yet. So who does the servicing, how fast, and where the spare parts are stocked must be in writing in the proposal before you buy; ask for it. What is already settled: for solar irrigation, the warranty is contractual and in writing (15 years panels, 5 inverter and control box, 2 pump) and spare parts stay available for 10 years. For the solar cold room, the warranties and parts come in writing in the proposal. Who makes it and who answers for it: the company. Ask for a proposal.

Who makes it, where is it made and who actually sells it to me?

The maker and supplier is Dutch Flow Technology PTE LTD, of Singapore, with the equipment made and assembled in Thailand; Agrotech da Holanda is the Brazilian brand for that technology. In Brazil, Regen Studio Consultoria LTDA (CNPJ 57.579.114/0001-55, São Paulo) is the commercial agent: it is not the seller and not the operator. In the name, “da Holanda” is the country, not a surname: the system was designed by Dutch founders with more than 20 years in the pump and water trade. Contact: info@agrotechdaholanda.com.br. Nothing is installed in Brazil yet (what is already running). See also what we do.

Is there a working system in Brazil I can visit?

Not yet: nothing is installed in Brazil. Dutch Flow Technology runs a solar irrigation system in San José, in the Philippines, and has a cooperation with the government of Ghana on smart solar irrigation (MoU, July 2026). The solar cold room? A new line for Dutch Flow Technology: the first units go to the first pilots. That is why we are looking for the first farms: the first pilot comes at cost price, with design and sensor data open to you, and help finding financing. See the pilot and, before you decide, who repairs it if it breaks.
