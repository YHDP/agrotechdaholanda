What we do

Two products for your farm

Smart solar irrigation

The pump runs on the sun and irrigation follows soil moisture.

The pump runs on the sun. Irrigation follows the soil, not the calendar.

Sensors measure soil moisture and the weather, and the control box starts the pump at the right moment. No fuel, and no waiting for the grid to arrive or release capacity.

  • No fuel
  • Soil-moisture sensors
  • Any type of irrigation

Solar cold room

A 6 or 12 metre container chills the harvest on the farm, with the sun first.

Harvest, chill on the farm, and sell when the price is right.

A container that arrives ready by truck, with a cold room and a technical bay. The cold comes from the sun first; at night, the battery holds some hours, and the grid or the generator covers the rest.

  • From −5 °C to +18 °C
  • Arrives by truck and can be moved
  • Production in 6 to 8 weeks

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a grid connection?

For irrigation, no: the solar pump works by day, on the sun, and needs no grid connection. The solar cold room also uses the sun first, but its installation includes a grid or generator connection as backup for the night. How that works hour by hour: the cold room at night. For irrigation, a grid-tie inverter is an option for when there is not enough sun (tech sheet).

How much does solar irrigation or the solar cold room cost?

There is no price list on the site: the price of solar irrigation and of the solar cold room comes in a detailed proposal, with no obligation. The calculator uses only an estimated cost per hectare of the system landed in Brazil, to show the payback. For irrigation it moves with area, water source, total head, crop and options; borehole, installation, freight and import are quoted separately, and payment is 50% at order and 50% before shipment. For the cold room it moves with size, options, freight, import and installation. The PDF tech sheet comes by e-mail, with no obligation. Ask for a proposal or see what you save on energy (calculator).

If it breaks, who repairs it? Are spare parts available in Brazil?

Today there is no service network of its own in Brazil: nothing is installed here yet. So who does the servicing, how fast, and where the spare parts are stocked must be in writing in the proposal before you buy; ask for it. What is already settled: for solar irrigation, the warranty is contractual and in writing (15 years panels, 5 inverter and control box, 2 pump) and spare parts stay available for 10 years. For the solar cold room, the warranties and parts come in writing in the proposal. Who makes it and who answers for it: the company. Ask for a proposal.

Who makes it, where is it made and who actually sells it to me?

The maker and supplier is Dutch Flow Technology PTE LTD, of Singapore, with the equipment made and assembled in Thailand; Agrotech da Holanda is the Brazilian brand for that technology. In Brazil, Regen Studio Consultoria LTDA (CNPJ 57.579.114/0001-55, São Paulo) is the commercial agent: it is not the seller and not the operator. In the name, “da Holanda” is the country, not a surname: the system was designed by Dutch founders with more than 20 years in the pump and water trade. Contact: info@agrotechdaholanda.com.br. Nothing is installed in Brazil yet (what is already running). See also what we do.

Is there a working system in Brazil I can visit?

Not yet: nothing is installed in Brazil. Dutch Flow Technology runs a solar irrigation system in San José, in the Philippines, and has a cooperation with the government of Ghana on smart solar irrigation (MoU, July 2026). The solar cold room? A new line for Dutch Flow Technology. That is why we are looking for the first farms: the first pilot comes at cost price, with design and sensor data open to you, and help finding financing. See the pilot and, before you decide, who repairs it if it breaks.
