We are on the Dutch trade mission to Brazil

From 27 September to 2 October 2026, a trade mission from the Netherlands travels through São Paulo and Belém to work on sustainable agriculture, water and biodiversity. Agrotech da Holanda takes part as a company.

Published on 25 September 2026

The mission

Its official English name is Dutch trade mission to sustainable agriculture, biodiversity, water and climate in Brazil. In Dutch: Handelsmissie Brazilië: kansen in duurzame landbouw, water en biodiversiteit.

The mission is organised by RVO, the Netherlands Enterprise Agency, with the Embassy of the Netherlands in Brasília and the Consulate-General in São Paulo (mission page, in Dutch).

The route runs through the city of São Paulo, the interior of São Paulo state and Belém, with field visits, meetings with research institutions such as Embrapa, and business matchmaking.

Solar irrigation and the solar cold room

We bring Dutch Flow Technology's technology to two questions every grower knows: where the water comes from, and what happens to the harvest after it leaves the field.

Water and energy. Smart solar irrigation pumps with power from panels on the farm itself and switches on by soil moisture. The payback calculator (in Portuguese) shows what diesel costs your irrigation today.

Cold after the harvest. The solar cold room cools on the farm, in a container that arrives ready. It is a new line from Dutch Flow Technology.

On the mission we want to hear from growers: where water is short, where diesel weighs on the budget, and where harvests are lost for want of cold.

Follow the mission

We will share the mission day by day in our Instagram stories, at @agrotech_da_holanda.
