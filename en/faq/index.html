About Agrotech da Holanda

What does Agrotech da Holanda do?

We represent Dutch Flow Technology's technology in Brazil, in two product lines: smart solar irrigation, which irrigates when the soil asks for it, and cooling containers, which chill the harvest on the farm itself.

Who is Dutch Flow Technology?

It is the manufacturer and supplier of the technology we represent, based in Singapore; the equipment is made and assembled in Thailand. Agrotech da Holanda is the brand under which that technology reaches the Brazilian farmer.

Are you a Brazilian company?

The commercial agent in Brazil is Regen Studio Consultoria LTDA, a Brazilian company based in São Paulo. The contact is info@agrotechdaholanda.com.br.

Why 'da Holanda'?

Because the system is designed by the Dutch, with more than 20 years of experience in the water sector, in the Dutch tradition of producing more with less water and less energy. Holanda is the Portuguese name of the Netherlands, and here it means the country, not the Brazilian surname.

Smart solar irrigation

How does the solar pump for irrigation work?

The solar panels feed a solar pump inverter, which drives the pump directly, with no diesel. The pump can be submersible, in a borehole, or a surface pump, in a river or pond. In the complete systems, the control box starts the pump when soil moisture asks for it.

Does it irrigate by itself?

In the complete systems, yes. The control box reads the sensors and starts the pump when the soil asks for it, instead of following a fixed calendar. You follow along and can operate it from your phone.

What does the system measure?

In the complete systems, sensors measure the soil, the water and the weather. What each sensor measures is in the tech sheet.

What size of area is it for?

The reference systems go from 1 to 10 ha. Larger areas, up to 30 ha per pump, are sized case by case.

Do I need a permit to take the water?

Yes. Taking water in Brazil requires a water-use permit (outorga) from the state or federal authority. The permit is the farmer's responsibility, and we help with the process from the first conversation.

How much does it cost?

There is no price on the site. The price varies with the area, the water source, the total head, the crop and the options, and comes in a detailed proposal, with no obligation.

What does diesel cost me today?

It depends on your area, your crop and the price you pay. The calculator (in Portuguese) does that sum with your own numbers.

Is a system already installed in Brazil?

Nothing is installed in Brazil yet. Dutch Flow Technology runs a system in San José, in the Philippines. We are looking for the first farms for a pilot; see how it works.

Cooling containers

Does the cooling container need grid power?

Not for day-to-day use. The sun cools the room and charges the battery; at night, the battery keeps the cold. When the battery reaches its set minimum, the system switches by itself to the grid or to a generator, if there is one, and recharges the battery.

What temperature does it reach?

From +10 °C to −5 °C. How many hours of operation a day −5 °C requires is in the tech sheet. We promise nothing below −5 °C.

How much fits inside?

The container holds the cold room and a technical bay with the batteries, the inverter and the condensing unit, so the cold room is smaller than the container. The inside dimensions are in the tech sheet; the exact dimensions of the cold room come with the proposal.

What is in the tech sheet?

One sheet per container size, as a PDF in English: dimensions, insulation, refrigeration, panels, inverter, battery, delivery and the settings table per product. Request it here; it arrives in your e-mail.

How much does it cost?

There is no price on the site. The price depends on the size, the options, freight, import and installation in Brazil. It comes in a detailed proposal, with no obligation.

What about the warranty and spare parts?

Warranty, spare parts and battery autonomy without sun are stated in the proposal, in writing.

Can the container be moved?

Yes. It arrives ready, by truck, and can change location. That is what lets one container serve several farms over the season.

Is one already installed in Brazil?

Nothing is installed in Brazil yet. We are looking for the first farms for a pilot; see how it works.

Cooperatives

Can a cooperative buy the container for its members?

Yes, and it is the use that makes the most sense. The cooperative buys and the members share, because each member's harvest falls on different days.

How is the schedule organized among the members?

Agreed before the season: by booking, paying per use, or with a schedule for the year. The best arrangement is the one the group can keep.

Does the container sit idle the rest of the year?

That is exactly what it avoids. A cold room owned by a single farmer sits idle most of the season; shared, it moves from one neighbor to the next.

Talking business

Why is there no price on the site?

Because the price depends on the area, the crop, the size and the layout, and a number out of context helps no one decide. Tell us what you grow and where, and we reply with a detailed proposal, with the price.

Do you visit the property?

Yes. Before sizing any system, we take measurements on site.

How do I start?

Request the tech sheet or a proposal on the product pages, or write to info@agrotechdaholanda.com.br saying what you grow, where the property is and which problem is most urgent: water, or losses after the harvest.
