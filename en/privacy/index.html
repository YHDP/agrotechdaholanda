Privacy Policy

Who is responsible for the data, what we keep when you request the tech sheet or a proposal, what we never collect and how to exercise your rights. This page is a translation of the Portuguese policy, which is the binding text; in case of doubt, the Portuguese version prevails.

The short version

  • No cookies. None for advertising and none for measurement.
  • Everything served from this domain: fonts, styles, scripts and video. No requests to third parties while you browse.
  • Cookie-free measurement: we count visits with a digest of the IP address that changes every day; the IP address itself is never stored, and raw events are deleted after 48 hours.
  • Your data stays in the European Union, on a server in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • We never sell data and we do not use your e-mail for mailings.

Three scenarios

You only visit the site

We ask for nothing and store nothing that identifies you. The aggregated measurement described in Cookie-free analytics counts the visit without knowing who you are.

You request the tech sheet

We store your e-mail, the product you chose, the language and the page you requested it from. With that we send the sheet and reply if you write back. The data stays in the EU and is not shared with third parties.

You request a proposal

We store your e-mail and the answers you yourself wrote in the form: crop, area or volume, water source, municipality, energy on site and the choices you make. If the proposal is prepared with Dutch Flow Technology, in Singapore, those answers go to them for that single purpose (see International transfer).

What we collect and why

Tech sheet request

  • Data: e-mail, product (smart solar irrigation or solar cold room), language, page address and the version of the consent text you accepted.
  • Purpose: to send the tech sheet and reply to this request.
  • Legal basis: your consent (LGPD, art. 7, I), given in the box you tick before sending.

Proposal request

  • Data: e-mail and the answers you write (crop, area or volume, water source, municipality, energy on site, size, form of use, irrigation type), plus the same technical data as the tech sheet request.
  • Purpose: to prepare a proposal for your area and reply to you.
  • Legal basis: your consent (LGPD, art. 7, I) and the preliminary steps of a contract you requested (art. 7, V).

Protection against abuse

The forms use a hidden field that only robots fill in, a minimum time to fill in the form and a small calculation done by your browser (proof of work). A visual challenge appears only when these checks point to risk. None of this identifies you, and this technical data is not stored. To limit the number of submissions, the server keeps for up to 24 hours an irreversible digest of your IP address combined with the date; the IP address itself is not stored. Legal basis: legitimate interest (LGPD, art. 10).

What we do NOT collect

  • No cookies, on any page.
  • No tracking pixels, browser fingerprinting or advertising scripts.
  • No data shared with advertisers, data brokers or social networks.
  • No profiling and no automated decisions.
  • No use of your e-mail for marketing. A mailing list, if one ever exists, will have its own box, separate and unticked.

Cookie-free analytics

Our own measurement counts which pages are read, so that we can improve what we write. It works like this:

  1. Your browser sends the page address, the event type, the domain you came from when it is another site, the previous page on this site (kept only in the tab, in sessionStorage, and deleted when the tab closes) and your time zone.
  2. The events are: page visit, scrolling to 25, 50, 75 and 100%, clicks on some buttons, start and milestones of the film, and the time on the page when you leave.
  3. The server turns your IP address and your browser into an irreversible digest with a random value that changes every 24 hours, only to count the day's visitors. The IP address itself is never stored, and the digest does not allow you to be followed from one day to the next.
  4. The time zone becomes a country code. The browser becomes only the device type (phone, tablet or computer) and the browser family.
  5. Raw events are deleted after 48 hours. Only the aggregated numbers remain.

Since there are no cookies and nothing is stored on your device beyond the open tab, the measurement does not require prior consent. The legal basis is legitimate interest (LGPD, art. 10): understanding the aggregated use of the site to improve the content.

Processors and storage

Processors (operadores) are the companies that process data on behalf of the controller.

  • Supabase: database and functions, in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Stores the requests and the aggregated measurement, and delivers the tech sheet links.
  • Lettermint: e-mail delivery, in the European Union. Delivers the sheet you requested and the confirmation of the request.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States): serves these pages. Like any web server, it receives your IP address when delivering the page, under GitHub's privacy policy.

The tech sheet sits in private storage. The link you receive is signed and valid for 14 days.

International transfer

The data stays in the European Union. There is one more transfer, and only for the proposal request: when the proposal is prepared with Dutch Flow Technology PTE LTD, in Singapore, the manufacturer of the equipment, we send them the answers you gave and your e-mail, for the sole purpose of preparing the proposal.

This transfer happens at your request, to carry out the preliminary steps of the contract you asked for (LGPD, art. 33, IX), and is stated prominently in the form before sending. The tech sheet request is not shared with anyone.

Who has access

Only the Regen Studio team that answers the requests, and Dutch Flow Technology in the case described above. Apart from them and the processors listed, no one receives your data, and it is never sold.

Retention

  • Tech sheet and proposal requests: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • IP digest to limit submissions: up to 24 hours.
  • Signed tech sheet links: valid for 14 days.
  • Raw measurement events: 48 hours; only the aggregated numbers remain.

If the conversation turns into a proposal or a contract, the e-mails exchanged then follow the legal retention periods of that contract.

Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18) you can, at any time:

  • confirm whether we process your data, and access it;
  • correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • request the anonymization, blocking or deletion of data that is unnecessary or processed in breach of the law;
  • request the portability of your data;
  • request the deletion of data processed with your consent;
  • know with whom we share your data;
  • know that you may refuse consent, and what happens in that case;
  • withdraw consent, without affecting what was done before.

If you are in the European Union, the GDPR gives you equivalent rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to the data protection authority of your country.

While you only read the site, there is no personal record to access or delete, because we store nothing that identifies you.

How to exercise them

Write to info@agrotechdaholanda.com.br from the e-mail you used in the request. We confirm that the request is yours before showing or deleting any data, and we reply within 15 days. You can also complain to the Brazilian data protection authority (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados, ANPD).

Children

The site is for farmers and cooperatives. We do not knowingly collect data from children. By sending a form, you confirm that you are 16 or older, or, in Brazil, 12 or older with parental consent. If we learn of a request made by a child, we delete the data.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date on this page changes with it, and the stored requests stay linked to the text that was accepted at the time.
