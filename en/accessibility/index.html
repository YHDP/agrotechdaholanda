Accessibility

We want anyone to be able to read this site, request the tech sheet and contact us, on a phone, on a computer or with assistive technology. This page is a translation of the Portuguese statement; in case of doubt, the Portuguese version prevails.

Updated on September 24, 2026

Our commitment

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA, and to the Brazilian Inclusion Act (Lei Brasileira de Inclusão, Law 13.146/2015, art. 63). We review every new page against these rules before publishing.

What we have done

  • Structure: page landmarks (header, navigation, content, footer), a single main heading per page and subheadings in order.
  • Keyboard: everything works with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus ring, and every page has a link to skip straight to the content.
  • Contrast: text with a contrast of at least 4.5:1 and interface elements of at least 3:1. The brand orange and blue are not used as text colors on a light background.
  • Forms: every field has a label; errors appear as text, are read by screen readers and point to the field.
  • Images and diagrams: alternative text on images with content; the cycle and nexus diagrams have a title and a description, and on a phone the cycle also comes as a list.
  • Touch: touch targets of at least 44 pixels on a phone.
  • Motion: anyone who asks the system for less motion gets no animations and no smooth scrolling.
  • No external dependencies: fonts and scripts served from this domain, with nothing from third parties in the way.

Known limitations

  • The water risk map (water risk by municipality, in Portuguese) draws the 5,570 municipalities on an interactive map that a screen reader cannot navigate. The search by name, on the same page, shows the same data as text.
  • The tech sheets in PDF may not fully meet the PDF/UA standard. If you need the content in another format, write to us.
  • The visual check on the forms appears only when the invisible checks point to risk: you carry a solar panel to the roof. It works with the keyboard; if it still blocks your submission, write directly to our e-mail.

Found a barrier?

Tell us what happened, on which page and, if you can, with which device or assistive technology: info@agrotechdaholanda.com.br. We reply within five working days and fix what is technically possible within 30 days.

Technical specifications

The site uses HTML, WAI-ARIA, CSS and a little JavaScript. It was tested in the current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, on phone and on computer.
