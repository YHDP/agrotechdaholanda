Energy

Sun first

Three sources, one controller. It uses the cheapest energy available and protects the temperature above all.

  1. 1 · Sun

    By day, the panels cool the room and charge the battery.

  2. 2 · Battery

    At night, the lithium iron phosphate battery holds the cold for some hours: about 5 hours in the worst case, on a 40 °C night, and longer on a normal night.

  3. 3 · Grid or generator

    When the battery reaches its set minimum, it takes over by itself and recharges the battery. For the grid and generators in Brazil, the 60 Hz version.

Who owns it

Three ways to own one

The container travels by truck, so it fits on a farm, in a cooperative or in the hands of whoever buys the produce.

  • One farm

    Owns and runs its own container. Works when the volume is steady through the season.

  • The cooperative

    One container shared among the members. What one alone cannot justify, a group can.

  • The aggregator

    The buyer owns it, collects from several farms, grades and ships.

How the cooperative shares it

The best arrangement is the one the group can keep.

  • Booking

    The member books it for a period and pays only the transport. Good for the first season.

  • Pay per use

    Per day or per ton. Whoever uses it more pays more, and that repays the investment.

  • Yearly schedule

    The calendar is agreed once, at the start of the season, as is already done with the tractor.

Frequently asked questions

Does the solar cold room need grid power?

It uses the sun first. By day, the sun cools the room and charges the battery. At night, the battery holds the cold for some hours: about 5 hours in the worst case, on a 40 °C night, and longer on a normal night. After that, the system switches by itself to the grid or to the generator and recharges the battery. That is why the installation includes a connection to the grid or to a generator, as backup. For the grid and generators in Brazil, the 60 Hz version.

What temperature does it reach?

From +10 °C to −5 °C. How many hours of operation a day −5 °C requires is in the tech sheet. We promise nothing below −5 °C.

How much fits inside?

The container holds the cold room and a technical bay with the batteries, the inverter and the condensing unit, so the cold room is smaller than the container. The inside dimensions are in the tech sheet; the exact dimensions of the cold room come with the proposal.

What is in the tech sheet?

One sheet per container size, as a PDF in English: dimensions, insulation, refrigeration, panels, inverter, battery, delivery and the settings table per product. Request it here; it arrives in your e-mail.

How much does it cost?

There is no price on the site. The price depends on the size, the options, freight, import and installation in Brazil. It comes in a detailed proposal, with no obligation.

What about the warranty and spare parts?

The battery has a 5-year warranty. The other warranties and the spare parts are stated in the proposal, in writing.

Can the container be moved?

Yes. It arrives ready, by truck, and can change location. That is what lets one container serve several farms over the season.

Is one already installed in Brazil?

Nothing is installed in Brazil yet. We are looking for the first farms for a pilot; see how it works.
