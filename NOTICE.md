## Principal's own footage (Instagram reels)

- **DFT controller cabinet** — from `Dutch Flow Irrigation Video Ph.mp4`, Dutch Flow Technology's own
  promotional film of a Philippines installation (supplied 16 July 2026). Not a third-party licence:
  our principal's material, used by its Brazilian sales agent. Used on the smart-control beat of the
  água reel.
  The installation is **VIOLAGO Smart Solar Irrigation Inc's**, the Philippines installer, and their
  branding appeared in four places on the cabinet and controllers. It is **removed**, not blurred:
  content-aware fill (Laplace diffusion from the surrounding surface plus a grain stamp), tracked
  across the camera move. Method and candidates: `social-post/labs/violago/`, script `fill.py`.
  Nothing of VIOLAGO's mark is shown, and nothing of ours is put in its place — the cabinet is left
  unbranded rather than re-badged, because re-badging a partner's installation would imply we built
  it. Whether to show VIOLAGO openly with proper credit instead is still an open question with Tom
  (`dft-violago-logo-clearance-2026-07-26`).

## Climate data (risco-hidrico-por-municipio.html)

- **Lobelia Earth, Brasil in a +2ºC World** — the municipal climate figures, the choropleth values and
  the geometry behind `risco-hidrico-por-municipio.html`. Initiative of **Lobelia** and **Instituto Itaúsa**,
  <https://portal.brasil2c.com/>. No formal licence is published; the platform's About page asks for
  attribution in this exact form:

  > Source: Lobelia Earth, Brasil in a +2ºC World, accessed on [date]

  with a link to the portal where possible, and asks that publicly-available work using it be shared
  with `brasil2c@lobelia.earth`. Both are honoured: the attribution and access date sit in the page
  footer, and the notification is Yvo's to send before the page goes live.
  The platform also states its results are **screening-level insights, not asset- or site-specific
  diagnostics**, and do not replace on-site assessment. That limitation is carried in the footer and in
  every ficha PDF; never drop it.
  Underlying sources credited by Lobelia: JRC Global River Flood Hazard Maps (LISFLOOD / LISFLOOD-FP),
  WRI Aqueduct Floods, IBGE landslide susceptibility, CHIRPS v3, bias-adjusted CORDEX projections,
  MapBiomas Collection 9, ESA CCI Burned Area.
  Geometry is simplified and the values are re-aggregated locally; the source rasters and tiles are not
  redistributed. Priority indices (IPI, ICF) are our own analysis, not Lobelia's.
